# Project Idea: Elle Flowers — Elegance Delivered

## Vision
**Elle Flowers** is a boutique digital florist offering premium, handcrafted bouquets with the speed of modern logistics. Our vision is to combine the timeless beauty of floral art with a seamless, high-tech purchasing experience.

## Core Identity
- **Brand name**: Elle Flowers.
- **Aesthetic**: Sophisticated, feminine, and clean. Leveraging a palette of lavender, purple, and soft rose.
- **Promise**: "Your ideal bouquet delivered in 60 minutes."

## Key Features
- **Express Delivery**: Guaranteed 60-minute delivery for urgent gestures.
- **Artisan Collections**: Floral compositions curated with emotion in mind.
- **Smart Catalog**: Easy filtering by flower type (Roses, Peonies, Mix) and quick-select options.
- **Premium Checkout**: A refined cart experience with clear summary and easy order modification.

## Tech Alignment
- **Purple & Lavender**: The primary brand colors, signifying luxury and creativity.
- **Emerald Green**: Used specifically for "Add to Cart" and successful transaction states to denote freshness.
- **Rose Pink**: Used for intuitive destructive actions like removal, maintaining a floral-consistent palette.
- **Script Typography**: Used for the brand logo to provide a personal, artisan touch.
